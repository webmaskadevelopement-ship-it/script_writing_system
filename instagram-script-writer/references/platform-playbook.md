# Platform playbook

## Instagram

Prioritize Reels, carousels, captions, visible comments, saves/shares when shown, and searchable caption/hashtag language. Study the first frame, first spoken line, on-screen text, loop potential, and profile-to-post promise. Keep the final script native to Instagram rather than copying another platform's pacing blindly.

## TikTok

Look for rapid premise delivery, comment-reply formats, recurring series, trends, and creator/audience language. Treat trend audio and memes as time-sensitive references; recommend an original equivalent when rights or availability are unclear.

## YouTube Shorts

Inspect title/thumbnail framing, spoken clarity, search intent, retention-oriented structure, and whether the short resolves a question. Adapt stronger explanatory beats to Instagram rather than importing long-form exposition.

## Facebook

Consider older or broader audiences, shareability, community context, and caption-led consumption. Avoid assuming Facebook engagement predicts Reels performance.

## Reddit and web sources

Use these for pain points, objections, vocabulary, and unanswered questions—not as direct evidence of Instagram performance. Preserve privacy: paraphrase and do not expose usernames unnecessarily.

## Cross-platform synthesis

Tag every finding with its source platform. A pattern repeated across platforms is a stronger creative hypothesis, but performance metrics remain platform-specific. When adapting, preserve the core audience tension and payoff while changing the hook, length, visuals, CTA, and caption for Instagram.
